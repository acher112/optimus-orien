## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This document delivers **Stage 0: Audit and Catalogue** for the Optimus Orien Badges and Achievements System.

### The Golden Rule
> *The existing platform is the source of truth. Award a badge only if its FULL criteria can be computed reliably from data Optimus Orien already stores or derives. No new data collection, no new forms, no estimated or substitute metrics, no silently weakened criteria: implement a badge completely or exclude it, with the reason. If a whole category has no existing module, skip the category; do not build the module.*

### Platform Audit Findings
1. **Academic Examinations & Finalized Results:**
   - Evaluated models: `CalculatedStudentResult`, `CalculatedSubjectResult`, `ExamConfiguration` (`status='published'`), and `ResultPublicationHistory`.
   - Results are strictly bound to published configurations (`status='published'`). Unfinalized or draft marks are never evaluated.
   - Improvement metrics can be calculated deterministically between consecutive published academic terms (`ExamConfiguration.academic_year` or `name` matching).

2. **Attendance & Excused-Absence Policy:**
   - Evaluated models: `Attendance` (students) and `StaffAttendance` (faculty/staff).
   - Valid statuses: `present`, `absent`, `late`, `leave`, `excused`.
   - Standard Optimus Orien attendance formula (from `backend/core/student_views.py`):
     $$\text{Denominator} = \max(\text{Total School Days} - \text{Excused Days} - \text{Approved Leave Days}, 0)$$
     $$\text{Attendance Rate} = \left(\frac{\text{Present Days} + \text{Late Days}}{\text{Denominator}}\right) \times 100$$
   - Excused absences and leaves are excluded from the denominator. Missing days are never treated as present. Minimum threshold of instructional days (e.g., $\ge 15$ days for monthly evaluations) is enforced.

3. **Homework Verification:**
   - Evaluated models: `Homework` and `HomeworkSubmission`.
   - A homework submission is verified if `status == 'completed'` and `confirmed_by__isnull=False` (confirmed by a teacher).
   - Verification percentage: $(\text{Verified Completed Submissions} / \text{Total Assigned Submissions}) \times 100$.

4. **Participation vs. Behaviour:**
   - Evaluated models: `Observation`.
   - Valid kinds: `kind in ('participation', 'skill')` with integer scale $1 \dots 10$. Supports the "Active Learner" badge ($\ge 8/10$ average).
   - Standalone behaviour incident/conduct tracking does not exist in standard academic tables.
   - `PsychologicalEvaluation` stores psychological notes and behavioural evaluations, but is **strictly protected** by Rule 7 ("Psychological Evaluation data is never used").
   - Consequently, all candidate badges requiring standalone behaviour tracking (`Culture Builder`, `Behaviour Mentor`, `Behaviour Champion`) are **EXCLUDED** under the Golden Rule.

5. **Teacher Assignments & Performance Benchmarks:**
   - Evaluated models: `ClassSubjectTeacher`, `TeachingAssignment`, `ClassTeacherAssignment`, `TeacherPerformanceCriteria`, `TeacherPerformanceAlert`.
   - Teachers are evaluated against published results for their assigned classes. The `TeacherPerformanceCriteria` engine classifies teachers into supportive statuses (`on_track`, `watch`, `needs_support`).
   - Custom HR numerical quota targets do not exist. Therefore, "Teacher Champion (80% of teachers meet targets)" is excluded unless formally approved to measure the percentage of teachers with `on_track` status.

6. **Quiz Competitions & Online Assessments:**
   - Evaluated models: `QuizCompetition` and `QuizAttempt`.
   - Filter criteria: Quizzes count towards badges **only** when `is_official=True` (official school/teacher olympiads). Peer-to-peer student challenges (`is_official=False`) are strictly excluded per Rule 3.
   - Fields present: `accuracy_percentage`, `score_percentage`, `time_spent_seconds`, `subject`, `country`, `submitted_at`.
   - Tournament brackets, multi-round playoffs, and multi-school 3-country tournaments do not exist in the database schema. Therefore, all tournament-specific badges are **EXCLUDED**.

7. **Roles, Permissions & Manual Awards:**
   - User roles: `platform_admin`, `organisation_admin`, `school_admin`, `principal`, `teacher`, `student`, `parent`.
   - Audit trail: `AuditEvent` logs actor, school, organisation, resource, and timestamp.
   - Hierarchy for manual awards:
     - `organisation_admin`: Can confer to Principals, Teachers, Students.
     - `principal` / `school_admin`: Can confer to Teachers and Students of their own campus.
     - `teacher`: Can confer to Students of their assigned classes.
     - `student`: Strictly cannot confer awards.
   - Manual awards require a mandatory text justification, record timestamp and grantor, carry `is_manual=True`, and **never** unlock a system badge.

8. **Tenant Isolation & Row-Level Security (RLS):**
   - All badge award and progress records will be partitioned by `school_id` with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (`ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`), with tenant isolation policies.
   - Badge definitions and criteria templates will live in platform-level tables documented as intentionally unscoped.
